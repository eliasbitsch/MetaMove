# MetaMove Bari demo - narration, single source of truth for voice and story card.
#
# Each [key] matches a beat in StoryDirector. The text under it is spoken (cloned voice,
# Qwen3-TTS) and shown on the story card. Lines starting with # are ignored.
# Regenerate one line only:  generate_bari.py <key>
# Pronunciation fixes for the voice (GoFa as one word, ...) live in generate_bari.py.
#
# The argument: a cobot may share the floor with people anyway - but then it has to crawl
# everywhere, all the time. MetaMove lets it run at full speed while nobody is near and
# slows it only when someone comes close: safety without giving up throughput.

[welcome]
We couldn't bring you to the factory, so we brought the factory to you. Welcome to MetaMove. This is an ABB GoFa, a collaborative robot, life-size. Right now, it is packing gear kits. Watch it work for a moment.

[story]
Cobots are usually kept slow everywhere, just in case someone comes close. Safe, but it wastes most of the robot. MetaMove adds a distance-based speed-scaling safety layer: it knows where you are, so the arm runs at full speed until you come near.

[tracking]
How does it know? The QR code on the post is the spatial anchor. The headset's own SLAM tracking finds it, and the code is measured to the robot base. So your head and hands are known relative to the robot, and that distance sets the speed.

[refill]
The tray is getting low. The next batch is waiting on the trolley behind you, in the orange box. Pick it up and load it into the rack, while the arm keeps working.

[slow]
Notice the arm slowing down as you come closer? That is distance-based speed scaling. The closer you are, the slower it moves. Nothing personal.

[standstill]
And now it stops. Nothing broke. You are inside its reach, and it would rather wait than meet you.

[recover]
Step back. Notice it does not rush off straight away? Slowing down may be abrupt. Speeding up is not.

[empty]
The first box is empty. The arm noticed and switched to the reserve box on the left, so the line keeps running. Take the empty box out at the front and put it on the return lane below. Watch the arm while your hands are close.

[guide]
The new box sits in a lane the arm has never seen. Instead of programming it, show it. Pinch the cyan sphere on the tool and bring it over the orange box. Roughly is enough. Then raise either hand, palm up, and a hand menu opens. Press Teach there once you are happy with the position.

[grab]
Steady. I will follow your hand anywhere, except into the rack. Someone has to be sensible here.

[aligned]
Found it, and lined up to the millimetre. You gave the intent, the machine added the precision.

[release]
The line runs again from the box you just showed it, at full speed now that you are clear. That is MetaMove: careful when you are close, fast when you are not, and taught without a single line of code.
