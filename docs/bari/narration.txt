# MetaMove Bari demo - narration, single source of truth for voice and story card.
#
# Each [key] matches a beat in StoryDirector. The text under it is spoken (cloned voice,
# Qwen3-TTS) and shown on the story card. Lines starting with # are ignored.
# Regenerate one line only:  generate_bari.py <key>

[welcome]
We couldn't bring you to the factory, so we brought the factory to you. This arm is building kits: an ABB GoFa, life-size, picking gears at full speed. Watch it work for a moment.

[story]
Arms like this used to stand behind a fence. The safety standard now lets them share the floor with you, on one condition: the machine has to know where you are.

[refill]
The tray is getting low. The next batch is waiting on the trolley behind you, in the orange box. Pick it up and load it into the rack, while the arm keeps working.

[slow]
Notice the arm slowing down as you come closer? It measures your distance all the time. The closer you are, the slower it moves.

[standstill]
It stopped. Not because anything broke. You are inside its reach.

[recover]
Step back. Notice that it does not pick up straight away? Slowing down is allowed to be abrupt. Speeding up is not.

[empty]
The robot has given up on the empty box. Take it out at the front and put it on the return lane below.

[guide]
The arm still believes in the old box. Pinch the glowing point on the tool and bring it over the new box, from where you stand. Roughly is enough. Then press Teach.

[aligned]
It found the box and lined itself up. You gave the intent, the machine added the precision.

[release]
Let go. The line runs again from the box you just showed it, at full speed now that the floor is clear. You never touched the arm.
